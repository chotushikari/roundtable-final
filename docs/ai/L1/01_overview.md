# Product Overview

RoundTable is an adaptive technical interview aid. Companies supply a JD, desired outcomes, optional questions, and two to five panel roles. The recruiter builder offers a fixed five-role, ten-minute finale showcase or a configurable adaptive interview with a 5–90 minute budget; those settings become server-validated definition data, not browser state. Candidates enter through single-use guest links and speak with a clearly disclosed AI panel. A human always makes the employment decision.

The recruiter workspace is guided by the real sequence—hiring bar, blueprint, candidates, and evidence review. Its templates, seniority selector, and focus chips populate stored blueprint fields, while its workflow controls navigate to the corresponding real API-backed operation. It never represents a recruiter-side progress indicator as live model scoring.

The MVP includes voice, transcript evidence, role handoffs, adaptive difficulty, code and system-design workspaces, MCP actions, and post-session assessment. A presentation-only digital panel stage shows an original AI host visual with active role and genuine voice-agent state by default. When configured, Protoface connects through Agora Generic Avatar and publishes a real talking-avatar video track into the same RTC channel. Neither mode receives candidate media, performs biometric analysis, or becomes assessment input. Automated hiring decisions remain out of scope.

The public homepage demonstrates the product through one pinned visual narrative: a single RoundTable core, five connected panel roles, a bounded one-question Agora voice sample, and a final cursor-aware companion scene. Candidates still enter real interviews only through signed invitation links.

Candidate invitation, live-call, workspace, completion, and transition screens use the same dark card-based visual system. The live room shows current panel context, time, transcript, and connection health without rendering a client-calculated role-answer counter.

Recruiters attach an optional text resume before publishing; candidates do not upload it from the public invitation. Completed reports emphasize evidence coverage, competency and panel summaries, transcript citations, workspace facts, and human follow-ups without producing a hiring verdict.
